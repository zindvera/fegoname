Cookie & Storage Policy

Last updated: November 2026

01. Our Minimalist Approach to Tracking

FegoName does not deploy marketing cookies, cross-site trackers, behavioral ad pixels, or third-party profiling scripts. We prioritize lightning-fast utility performance and customer privacy by relying primarily on standard modern browser client storage (localStorage) to maintain your state and preferences.

02. Local Storage Keys We Utilize

Instead of storing your keys and licenses on our servers, FegoName keeps all working data directly on your local device. Below is a comprehensive breakdown of the exact storage items placed in your browser:

fegoname_encoded_groq User Controlled

Stores your obfuscated personal Groq API key when entered in Settings. Stored exclusively on your machine so you do not need to re-enter your key across searches.

fegoname_license_key Authentication

Holds your Gumroad Lifetime Deal or subscription license code to remember your Pro tier verification between visits.

fegoname_license_status & timestamp Cache State

Caches license validation states (active or invalid) and epoch timestamps to avoid making redundant network verification requests on every page load.

fegoname_limit_locked_until Quota Sync

Stores local rate limit expiration timers (24-hour sync) when free trial quotas on an IP address have reached the maximum free threshold.

03. Edge Infrastructure Cookies

While FegoName sets zero promotional cookies directly, our edge network provider, Cloudflare, may transmit strictly necessary technical cookies (such as security verification, bot mitigation tokens, or load balancing routing tokens like __cf_bm or cf_clearance).

These cookies are vital for DDoS defense, malicious bot filtration, and maintaining secure SSL handshakes. They cannot be turned off as they are mandatory for delivering safe, high-speed edge compute.

04. How to Manage or Clear Your Stored Data

Because all functional client keys are saved locally, you retain complete authority over your footprint:

  • Via FegoName: Open the API Key modal on the home page and click "Remove Key" to immediately purge your saved Groq key from local storage.
  • Via Your Browser: You can purge all stored licenses, quotas, and preferences at any time by opening your browser settings and clearing site data/cookies for this domain.

05. Policy Updates & Inquiries

Any modifications to how we handle local storage or technical cookies will be reflected directly on this page with an updated timestamp. If you have any technical questions regarding our storage practices, feel free to submit an inquiry through our Contact Page.
