1. Before Using & Plans

What makes FegoName different from regular AI naming tools? +

Most brand name generator tools just throw generic words together that are already registered everywhere. FegoName gives a list of really unique brand or product names with meaning, with no name taken on any domain extension and a high chance of no relevant Google search results. It uses our own secret name-building system that gives really unique, untaken brand or product names.

How does the free trial work? +

You get 3 complete free naming searches with full verification. When searching, FegoName continues checking in the background until it finds untaken names. After your free trial, you can unlock full unlimited searches with a license code.

What is the Lifetime Deal and how do I receive it? +

The Lifetime Deal gives you permanent access with a single payment on Gumroad. You receive your license key immediately upon checkout. Just click "Upgrade" in the app, paste the code, and click "Activate".

2. During Searches & How It Works

Why does FegoName ask for a free Groq API key? +

Groq provides a 100% free account that includes up to 14,400 fast requests every single day. Setting it up takes less than 30 seconds (just sign in with Google and copy your key). This allows you to generate names with ultra-fast speed without ever waiting in queues or experiencing delays.

Is my AI key and client brief safe? +

Yes, completely. Your key stays stored solely inside your personal browser on your computer. It is never saved on our databases or shared with third parties. Your client briefs are used solely to generate the names for your project and are never sold or logged.

How does the live domain check work? +

As names are coined, FegoName queries live web domain records across 14 extensions (.com, .net, .org, .io, .co, .ai, .app, .dev, .xyz, and more). If even a single extension is already registered, that name is discarded. Only names that are completely untaken across all tested extensions make it to your screen.

3. Handing Names Over to Clients

Do I own the names generated for my freelance projects? +

Yes, 100%. Any name and rationale you discover is completely yours to pitch, sell, or deliver to your clients. There are no licensing fees, royalties, or ownership claims by FegoName.

Why are one-click Google & Domain buttons included? +

Every generated card includes a one-click Google Title Check and a Domain Check button. This lets you quickly confirm with your own eyes that the name has no existing search footprint before presenting it to your client.

Can I copy the brand rationale directly into my client presentation? +

Yes! Each suggestion includes a ready-to-use breakdown explaining why the name was crafted, which phonetic elements were blended, and how it communicates your client's core value proposition—ready to paste straight into Figma decks or proposals.
