Frequently Asked Questions

1. Before Using & Plans

How is FegoName different from other name generators? +

Most name generators suggest common words that are already taken. FegoName creates fresh, unique brand and product names with a clear meaning—and checks that the domain name is completely available before showing it to you.

Can I try it for free before paying? +

Yes. You get 3 free searches to test everything out. After that, you can upgrade to unlock unlimited searches.

How do I activate my license after buying? +

Right after purchase, you receive a license code. Just click the "Upgrade" button at the top of the page, paste your code, and click "Activate".

2. Using FegoName

Why do I need a free Groq key, and is it hard to set up? +

Groq gives you a 100% free account with thousands of fast searches every day, so you never have to wait in line. Setting it up takes 30 seconds: sign in with Google on Groq, click "Create API Key", and paste it into FegoName.

Is my Groq key and what I type kept private? +

Yes. Your Groq key is saved only inside your own browser on your computer—we never store it or see it. Anything you type to describe a business or product is only used to create your names and is never shared with anyone.

Are the suggested domain names really untaken? +

Yes. Before showing any name, FegoName checks it across 14 popular domain endings like .com, .net, .org, .io, .co, and .ai. If even one of them is already registered by someone else, FegoName skips that name and finds another one for you.

3. Sharing Names with Clients

Can I sell or deliver these names to my clients? +

Yes, 100%. Every name and explanation you generate is yours to use in paid client projects with no extra fees or restrictions.

How do I double-check a name before sending it to a client? +

Every name card has a "Google Check" button and a "Domain Check" button. One click opens Google search results or a domain registrar so you can confirm everything yourself in seconds.

Does FegoName explain what each name means? +

Yes. Each name comes with a short, easy-to-read explanation of how the name was formed and why it fits the business or product—so you can easily explain your picks to your client.
