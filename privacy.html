Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

01. Our Privacy-First Commitment

At FegoName, your confidential client concepts, business briefs, and sensitive API credentials are treated with extreme care. We operate on a zero-tracking, privacy-first architecture designed to store only the bare technical minimum required to enforce fair rate limits and verify paid licenses.

02. Local Storage of Groq API Keys (BYOK)

Zero Server Database Storage

When you enter your personal Groq API key (gsk_...), it is encoded and saved strictly in your local web browser's storage (localStorage). It is never written to a permanent backend database, logged to persistent disks, or shared with advertisers.

During active search sessions, your key is transmitted via an encrypted HTTPS header (x-groq-key) directly to our stateless Cloudflare Worker, which instantly proxies the prompt directly to Groq's high-speed inference engine.

03. Business Briefs & Prompt Processing

The business briefs, product concepts, and exclude lists submitted via FegoName are processed ephemerally in RAM memory at Cloudflare edge data centers.

  • We do not build commercial training profiles or retain your raw ideas for model retraining.
  • Prompt contents are sent to the AI inference provider solely to generate the batch of candidate names requested.
  • Once candidate names are verified and returned to your browser screen, the memory buffer in the Worker is dismissed.

04. IP Quota Tracking via Cloudflare KV

For evaluation users who do not provide a personal Groq key or active Gumroad license, our backend records the incoming connecting IP address (cf-connecting-ip) in Cloudflare KV strictly to enforce the free trial allowance (3 lifetime searches).

These counters are set with an automated expiration TTL (time-to-live) of 30 days and are never cross-referenced with your personal identity, browsing history, or external identity databases.

05. License Verification via Gumroad

When you activate or renew a Lifetime Deal or annual license, your license key is forwarded through our Cloudflare Worker to Gumroad's secure licensing API (api.gumroad.com/v2/licenses/verify) to confirm payment status, chargebacks, or refunds. FegoName does not collect or process credit card numbers or banking data; all payment billing occurs directly on Gumroad's PCI-compliant infrastructure.

06. Third-Party Service Providers

Our service leverages reputable infrastructure partners for edge delivery and DNS verification:

  • Cloudflare: Edge compute (Workers), KV storage, and DNS-over-HTTPS queries to determine live domain name availability.
  • Groq: LPUs and model inference executing the naming logic.
  • Gumroad: Digital merchant of record for license purchases and verification.

07. Your Privacy Rights & Deletion

Because your Groq API key and license activation status reside in your device's browser, you can permanently erase them at any time simply by clicking "Remove Key" in the settings modal or by clearing your browser cache and site storage.

If you have questions regarding your data or privacy practices, please contact us through our Contact Page.
